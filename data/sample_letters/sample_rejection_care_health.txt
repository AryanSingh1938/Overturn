REASON FOR REPUDIATION:

Dear Madam,

We refer to the reimbursement claim documents submitted towards your recent hospitalization for the management of Lumbar Disc Herniation with Radiculopathy.

Our Medical In-House Assessment Team has scrutinized the hospitalization records, MRI reports, and treatment sheets. Following an internal audit:

1. Non-Active Line of Treatment (Medical Necessity):
As per Policy Terms & Conditions, Section 5, Clause 5.14 (Code-Excl04):
"Expenses related to any admission primarily for diagnostic and evaluation purposes only are excluded. Any hospitalization which is not medically necessary or where the patient could have been managed conservatively in an outpatient / OPD setting is outside coverage scope."

The indoor clinical notes indicate that conservative intravenous analgesics and physiotherapy were administered without any surgical intervention. The hospital stay was deemed not medically necessary as conservative spine management is clinically feasible on an OPD / daycare basis.

2. Violation of Intimation Timeline:
The hospitalization occurred on 15-OCT-2024; however, the initial claim intimation was received on 26-OCT-2024, exceeding the mandatory 48-hour intimation requirement under General Condition 3.1.

Accordingly, the competent authority has repudiated the claim in full.
