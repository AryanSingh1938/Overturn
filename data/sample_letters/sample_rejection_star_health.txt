SUB: REPUDIATION OF CASHLESS & REIMBURSEMENT CLAIM UNDER HEALTH OPTIMA POLICY

We refer to your claim submitted for the hospitalization of Mr. Ramesh Kumar Sharma at Max Super Speciality Hospital for the treatment of Acute Coronary Syndrome / CAD - NSTEMI.

Upon perusal of the claim documents, admission notes, and indoor case papers, the treating doctor has recorded a history of Essential Hypertension and Type-II Diabetes Mellitus for the past 6 years.

As per Section 4 - Exclusions, Clause 4.2 of the Policy Terms and Conditions:
"Pre-existing diseases (Code-Excl01): Expenses related to the treatment of a pre-existing disease (PED) and its direct complications shall not be covered until the expiry of 48 months of continuous coverage after the date of inception of the first policy."

The policy inception date was 12-AUG-2018, and this policy has been renewed continuously. However, our medical audit panel has noted that at the time of proposal inception, the insured failed to disclose a history of high blood pressure in the medical questionnaire. 

In view of the non-disclosure of material facts at the time of proposal, the company is not liable to pay any compensation under Section 4.2 of the Policy. Hence, we regret to inform you that your claim stands REPUDIATED.
